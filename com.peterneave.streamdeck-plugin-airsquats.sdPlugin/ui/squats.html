How it works

A beep sounds and the key flashes at this many minutes past every hour (0 = on the hour). Press the key to acknowledge. Hold it for 0.7 seconds to add 5 minutes.

Press the key when no reminder is due to preview how it looks and sounds. Custom sounds play for up to 10 seconds. WAV works everywhere; MP3, M4A and others work too. If the file goes missing, the default sound is used.
